Theoretical & computational statistical physics

Wei Wang researches stochastic processes and anomalous diffusion, including fractional stochastic dynamics, random time changes, stochastic resetting, and first-passage phenomena.

His work is motivated by a simple question: how can we understand complex motion when randomness, memory, and nonequilibrium effects are inseparable?

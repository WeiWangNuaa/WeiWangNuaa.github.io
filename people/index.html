Wei Wang 王炜

I am a statistical physicist studying anomalous diffusion in soft matter and biological systems. I use analytical models and numerical simulations to understand how memory and fluctuations in diffusivity shape particle motion.

Research

In a single-particle tracking experiment, when does a time average along one trajectory agree with an average over many particles? I study this question using fractional Brownian motion, random time changes, and stochastic resetting. These models let me vary the memory of the motion or the rate at which it unfolds and trace how each affects aging and ergodicity.

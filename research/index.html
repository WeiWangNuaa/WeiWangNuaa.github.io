RESEARCH

Randomness as a
physical principle.

I am interested in the mathematical structure and physical consequences of random dynamics, especially when transport is non-Gaussian, non-Markovian, or far from equilibrium.

01 / MOTION & MEMORY
∿

Anomalous diffusion & fractional dynamics

How do long-lived correlations, viscoelastic effects, and fluctuating diffusivity alter transport? I work with fractional Brownian motion, fractional Langevin processes, and heterogeneous stochastic models to understand observables such as displacement, increments, and time averages.

02 / RESETTING & RESPONSE
⟳

Stochastic resetting, search & control

Resetting protocols create new timescales and change relaxation, first-passage statistics, and nonlinear response. I study the interplay between stochastic interventions, deterministic dynamics, and external forcing, with a focus on control and signal amplification.

03 / RANDOM CLOCKS
τ

Subordination & non-Markovian dynamics

Random operational clocks connect tractable parent processes with complex, memory-bearing dynamics. My research examines when a fluctuating clock can be replaced by its mean and when the full distribution is essential, including applications to non-Markovian Fokker–Planck equations.

Single-particle trajectories & ergodicity

What can trajectories reveal about the process that generated them? I examine the roles of mean-squared increments, time averages, and ensemble averages in identifying genuine ergodicity breaking and distinguishing different classes of stochastic dynamics.
